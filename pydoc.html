Sharpe ratio, before costs, of trading each lookback window on its own. For a window (a, b) every contract is held in proportion to its move from b to a bars ago (in volatility units, capped at +-`cap`), at equal risk, from `lag` days later. Positive means the window predicts continuation, negative a reversal.
